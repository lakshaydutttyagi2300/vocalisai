# How to use this app

Welcome! This app is your personal school for **Python** and **SQL**. It runs only on your computer: your
progress is saved on your computer, and you do not need the internet or any paid service.

## The menu (left side)

On a computer the menu is on the left. On a narrow screen (like a phone) it is hidden - tap the **›** or **☰**
symbol in the top-left corner to open it.

| Menu item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Where you stopped, what to do next, what to revise |
| **Python Academy** / **SQL Academy** | All lessons, organised in levels |
| **Projects & Challenges** | Bigger practical tasks that combine several lessons |
| **Python Practice Lab** | Write and run any Python code; all Python exercises in one place |
| **SQL Practice Lab** | Explore sample databases and run any SQL |
| **Quizzes & Assessments** | Revision quizzes, lesson quizzes and level assessments |
| **Progress & Revision** | Your results, what you have mastered, what needs revision |
| **Knowledge Base** | Search everything: lessons, glossary, error guides, FAQ, your notes |
| **Notes & Bookmarks** | Your own notes and saved lessons/articles |
| **AI Tutor** | Optional - only if you choose to set one up |
| **Backup & Restore** | Protect your progress (and move it to a new computer) |
| **Settings** | Your name, time limits, pass mark, backup folder |
| **Help & Troubleshooting** | These pages |

## How a lesson works

Every lesson has four tabs:

1. **📖 Learn** - read the explanation, the analogy, the examples and the common mistakes. At the bottom,
   press **I have read this lesson**.
2. **✍️ Practise** - exercises at three levels: 🌱 beginner, 🌿 intermediate, 🌳 challenge. Type code into the
   box, press **▶ Run** to see what happens, and **✓ Check my answer** when you think it is right.
   - Stuck? Press **💡 Show a hint** - hints appear one at a time.
   - Still stuck? The **🔑 Solution** is there, but try first: exercises solved after viewing the solution
     count only half towards your mastery score.
3. **✅ Quiz** - short multiple-choice and typed questions. You see an explanation for every question.
4. **📝 Revise & next** - revision notes, your completion checklist, your notes, and the next lesson.

A lesson is **completed** when you have read it, passed its quiz (70% by default) and passed all its beginner
exercises. Completed lessons come back as short **reviews** after 1, 3, 7, 14... days so you do not forget them.

## Typing code

- The **Tab** key inserts 4 spaces (Python uses spaces at the start of lines to group code).
  **Shift+Tab** removes them. To leave the code box using only the keyboard, press **Esc** and then **Tab**.
- Pressing **Enter** keeps the indentation of the line above, and adds 4 spaces after a line ending in `:`.
- If your program uses `input()`, a box called **Input for your program** appears: type one answer per line.

## Where is my work saved?

Automatically, on your computer, in the app's `data` folder. Close the browser tab or the app at any time;
next time you start the app, everything is still there. Make backups regularly (see **Backup & Restore**).

## Starting and stopping the app

- **Start:** double-click `start_windows.bat` (Windows) or run `start_mac_linux.sh` (Mac/Linux). Your browser
  opens the app.
- **Stop:** close the black terminal window, or click inside it and press **Ctrl + C**.
- Closing only the browser tab does *not* stop the app - you can reopen it at `http://localhost:8501`.
